Indian Statutory Due Dates Tracker

Core recurring deadlines under Indian tax and corporate laws.

Monthly Due Dates

  • 7th: TDS & TCS payment deposit
  • 11th: GSTR-1 outward supply return
  • 15th: PF & ESI monthly contributions
  • 20th: GSTR-3B summary tax return

Quarterly Due Dates

  • 15th Jun/Sep/Dec/Mar: Advance Tax installments
  • 31st Jul/Oct/Jan/May: Quarterly TDS returns (24Q/26Q)
  • 22nd/24th of Month: QRMP GSTR-3B filings

Annual Milestones

  • 31st July: Income Tax Return (Non-Audit)
  • 30th September: Tax Audit Report u/s 44AB
  • 31st October: Corporate ITR-6 (Audit cases)
  • 31st December: GSTR-9 / 9C Annual Reconciliation
