FIRM OVERVIEW

Precision in Accounting. Integrity in Practice.

Established in Ongole, Andhra Pradesh, Kiran & Co is an independent Chartered Accountancy firm registered with the Institute of Chartered Accountants of India (ICAI).

WHO WE ARE

Partner-led financial stewardship since 2011.

We provide companies with experienced, partner-level financial and tax leadership, ensuring compliance burdens are handled with complete accuracy.

Our team comprises Fellow and Associate Chartered Accountants, information systems auditors (DISA), and corporate legal advisers. We serve over 650 corporate and mid-market clients across India.
