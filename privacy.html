Privacy Policy & Data Protection

Last updated: October 2026. Kiran & Co is committed to upholding the highest standards of data privacy, confidentiality, and statutory information security.

1. Scope & Commitment

Kiran & Co (“we”, “our”, or “the Firm”) is an ICAI-registered partnership firm of Chartered Accountants in Bengaluru, India. This Privacy Policy outlines our practices regarding the collection, processing, and protection of personal and corporate data collected via our website or during the execution of professional engagements.

2. Information We Collect

When you interact with our website or submit an inquiry via our client portal, we collect:

  • Contact Information: Full name, professional title, corporate email address, and telephone number.
  • Engagement Information: Nature of the business entity, requested practice area, and project brief details.
  • Technical Browsing Data: Standard server logs, IP addresses, and user-agent details required for network security and site stability.

3. Lawful Basis & Purposes of Processing

We process collected data in accordance with the Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act 2023, the Information Technology Act 2000, and the Chartered Accountants Act 1949:

  • To evaluate, respond to, and establish professional client engagements.
  • To satisfy mandatory Know-Your-Customer (KYC) and Anti-Money Laundering (AML) statutory compliance.
  • To communicate critical statutory tax and compliance regulatory alerts.

4. Client Privilege & Absolute Confidentiality

Under the regulations of the Institute of Chartered Accountants of India (ICAI), all financial records, tax disclosures, audit working papers, and proprietary corporate documents shared with our Firm are treated under strict professional secrecy and legal privilege. We do not sell, rent, or disclose client data to any commercial third parties.

5. Information Security & Encryption

We implement administrative, technical, and physical safeguards including SSL/TLS 256-bit encryption in transit, encrypted offline backups, role-based access restrictions, and dual-factor authentication on all practice management systems.

6. Data Principal Rights

You have the right to request access to your personal information held by our firm, request rectification of inaccurate records, or seek deletion of non-statutory communication records. For data requests, contact our Compliance Officer at privacy@kiranandco.com.
