QUALITY CONTROL

The Four-Eye Governance Principle

Dual-partner review on all formal statutory certificates and reports.

Under our internal quality control policy, every audit report, Tax Audit Form 3CD, transfer pricing certification, and statutory declaration requires review and sign-off by a concurring independent partner before release.

All partners and audit staff maintain mandatory annual Continuing Professional Education (CPE) hours as prescribed by the ICAI, ensuring strict compliance with evolving auditing standards.
