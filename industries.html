SECTOR SPECIALIZATION

Industry-Specific Accounting & Regulatory Focus

Taxation frameworks, revenue recognition models, and GST rate structures vary by sector. We provide tailored Indian CA advisory.

Sectors We Advise

Manufacturing & Engineering

Complex inventory costing, multi-plant operations, and capital goods tax planning.

  • Raw material & WIP inventory valuation (AS-2 / Ind AS 2)
  • GST Input Tax Credit on capital goods & job-work rules
  • Consortium bank audit and working capital monitoring

Technology & SaaS Platforms

Software exports, cross-border revenue, and equity capitalization.

  • SaaS milestone revenue recognition under Ind AS 115
  • Software export filings (SOFTEX, LUT, zero-rated GST)
  • Transfer Pricing audit (Form 3CEB) for overseas entities

Real Estate & Construction

Project accounting, JDA development agreements, and RERA certifications.

  • Percentage of Completion (PoCM) project-level accounting
  • GST on Joint Development Agreements & commercial leases
  • RERA designated bank account quarterly CA certificates

Healthcare & Life Sciences

Hospital chains, diagnostic labs, and medical equipment distributors.

  • Exempt healthcare services vs. taxable ancillary revenue
  • Doctor consultancy TDS withholding (Section 194J) management
  • Pharmaceutical inventory batch & expiry reconciliations

Retail, FMCG & E-Commerce

High transaction volume reconciliation, marketplace settlements, and sales tax.

  • Amazon / Flipkart payment settlement & TCS matching
  • Multi-state GST warehouse registrations & e-way bills
  • Trade discount, rebate, and credit note tax treatment

Startups & Scale-ups

DPIIT recognition, cap table accounting, and cross-border fundraising.

  • DPIIT Startup India tax exemptions u/s 56(2)(viib)
  • FDI reporting with RBI (FC-GPR and FLA returns)
  • Convertible Notes (CN), CCPS accounting, and 409A/DCF valuation
